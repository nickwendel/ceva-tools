A transaction is one confirmed warehouse task line (status C), counted on its confirmation date. Type comes from the warehouse process category when the export has it, otherwise from the activity code. Units use the actual destination quantity. Associate is the Confirmed By user. Hours run from an associate’s first to last confirmation each day, and lines per hour is blank under 30 minutes. When the export has no exception codes, lines where the actual quantity differs from the planned quantity are listed instead. Week is the last 7 days and Month the last 30 days, both including today. Loading an export replaces every day that appears in it, so load whole days. Open tasks are the lines with a blank or B status in the most recent export. Their age runs from Created On and Created At to the latest activity time in that file, shown amber from 4 hours and red from 8 hours.
