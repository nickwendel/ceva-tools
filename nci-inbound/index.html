Account

Notifications

With alerts on, this browser shows a notification when an issue lands on your company or the other company posts an update. The tab title also shows how many items need your company. Alerts are per device; turn them on wherever you work.

Access requests

People who tapped “Request access” on the sign-in screen. Approving creates their login’s role right away; they’re let in on their next refresh. Declining removes the request and leaves them without access.

People

Most people should use “Request access” on the sign-in screen and get approved above. To add someone by hand, create their login in the Firebase console under Authentication → Users → Add user, then paste the User UID here. The company decides which items count as “Needs us” and which alerts they get.
