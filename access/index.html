Access requests

People who tapped “Request access” on any tool. Pick a user group and approve; they’re let in right away, even if they left the page open. Decline removes the request.

People

Everyone with access. A person’s user group decides what they can do in each tool. Change it here and it takes effect at once. Company is only used by the inbound exceptions tool, to show which side the person is on.
